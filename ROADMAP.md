# Roadmap — Future Games

Candidate games for Simple Honest Games. All ideas should stay age-appropriate (4–6),
mistake-friendly, and built as single self-contained `.html` files following the design
system in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Add next (strong candidates)

- **First Sound** — see a picture (🐶), pick the letter it starts with. (early reading)
- **Alphabet Order** — put letters in A–B–C order. (early reading)
- **Shape Sorter** — sort or name shapes (circle, square, triangle, star). (fills the math/geometry gap)
- **Connect the Dots** — tap numbered dots in order to reveal a picture. (pairs with the new coloring/drawing direction)

## Reading / Letters

- **First Sound** — see a picture (🐶), pick the letter it starts with.
- **Word Family Builder** — swap the first letter to make new words (cat → hat → bat).
- **Opposites Match** — pair up opposites (big/small, hot/cold) with emoji.
- **Syllable Clap** — tap how many "claps" (syllables) a word has.

## Math / Numbers

- **Shape Sorter** — sort or name shapes (circle, square, triangle, star).
- **Bigger or Smaller** — pick which number/group is more.
- **Number Tracing** — like Letter Tracing but for digits (could fold into `letter-tracing.html`).
- **Connect the Dots** — tap numbered dots in order to reveal a picture.
- **Coin/Clock Basics** — simple "what time is it" o'clock matching.

## Logic / Visual

- **Spot the Difference** — find what changed between two emoji scenes.
- **Odd One Out** — pick the item that doesn't belong in a group.
- **Maze Runner** — drag a finger through a simple maze.
- **Sorting Bins** — drag items into categories (animals vs. food vs. toys).

## Creative / Calm

- **Coloring** — ✅ shipped (`coloring.html`): tap-to-fill animal sheets.
- **Sticker Scene** — drag emoji "stickers" onto a background to make a picture.
- **Make a Face** — mix and match eyes/nose/mouth to build silly faces.
- **Music Maker** — tap colored pads that play simple tones.
